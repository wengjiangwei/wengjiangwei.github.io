About Me

I am currently a Ph.D. student at PCALab, Nanjing University of Science and Technology, supervised by Prof. Jun Li. I obtained my M.S. degree in Computer Science in 2021 from Huzhou University, advised by Prof. Yunliang Jiang.

My research focuses on low-level vision, including image and video enhancement, and 3D reconstruction for real-world applications.

Previously, I worked on fuzzy systems and interpretable modeling for epileptic EEG recognition.
